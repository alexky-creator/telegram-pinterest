Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 3, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how Pinterest Scheduler for Telegram ("we", "our", or "the Application") collects, uses, and protects your data when you use our service to connect your Telegram content with your Pinterest account.

By using this Application, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Pinterest Data via API

When you authorize our Application to access your Pinterest account, we collect:

  • User Profile Data: Your Pinterest User ID, username, and profile URL
  • Board Information: Names, IDs, and descriptions of your Pinterest boards
  • Pin Metadata: Pin IDs, titles, and descriptions (only for Pins you create through our app)
  • OAuth Tokens: Access tokens and refresh tokens for API authentication

2.2 Telegram Data

We process the following data from your Telegram channel:

  • Media Content: Images you choose to publish to Pinterest
  • Text Content: Post captions you select for Pin descriptions
  • Channel Metadata: Channel ID for linking your Telegram and Pinterest accounts

2.3 Technical Data

  • IP address (for security and rate limiting)
  • Device type and browser information
  • Application usage logs (timestamps of API calls)

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data solely for the following purposes:

  • Core Functionality: To create Pins on your Pinterest boards based on your manual selections
  • Authentication: To maintain your Pinterest API connection via OAuth 2.0
  • Board Display: To show you a list of your boards when selecting where to publish
  • Rate Limiting: To prevent abuse and comply with Pinterest API usage limits
  • Error Handling: To provide you with meaningful error messages if something goes wrong

4. Data Storage and Security

4.1 What We Store

  • OAuth Tokens: Stored encrypted in our database
  • User-Application Link: Association between your Telegram ID and Pinterest User ID
  • Usage Logs: Kept for 30 days for debugging purposes

4.2 What We Do NOT Store

  • Your Pinterest password (we use OAuth, never ask for passwords)
  • Pin image files (images are uploaded directly from Telegram to Pinterest)
  • Complete Pin content after publishing
  • Your Telegram messages or channel content

4.3 Security Measures

  • All data transmission uses HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest using AES-256
  • Database access is restricted and monitored
  • Regular security audits and updates

5. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do NOT:

  • Sell your data to any third parties
  • Share your Pinterest data with advertisers
  • Use your content for training AI models
  • Transfer data to data brokers

We only share data with:

  • Pinterest API: To create Pins on your behalf (as authorized by you)
  • Telegram API: To retrieve the content you select for publishing

6. Your Rights and Control

6.1 Revoking Access

You can revoke our Application's access to your Pinterest account at any time:

  • Visit pinterest.com/settings/security
  • Find "Pinterest Scheduler for Telegram" in the Apps section
  • Click "Remove Access"

14. Specific Pinterest Data Handling

Pinterest API Scope Usage:

  • boards:read: Used only to display your board list in the app interface
  • pins:write: Used only when you explicitly click "Publish Pin" button
  • boards:write: (If requested) Used only if you choose to create new boards

We do NOT:

  • Access your private messages on Pinterest
  • Read or modify Pins created by other applications
  • Access your followers or following lists
  • Interact with other users on your behalf
  • Use Pinterest data for analytics or research purposes
